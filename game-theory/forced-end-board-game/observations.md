Observations:

1. Symbol choice does not seem to provide a long-term advantage.
   Since both players can place both 0 and 1, early losses
   can usually be avoided by choosing the opposite symbol.

2. For small board sizes (such as 3×3, 4×4, and 5×5),
   the game consistently reaches a state where no safe moves remain.

3. In such forced-end states, every possible move
   creates a winning row, causing the current player to lose.

4. Even when both players try to avoid losing,
   the game does not appear to allow a draw.

5. Across all tested cases, the outcome depends only on
   which player is forced to make the final move,
   not on specific tactical decisions.

6. If m × m is odd, the first player appears to be
   forced to make the final move and loses.

7. If m × m is even, the second player appears to be
   forced to make the final move and loses.

These points are observations, not formal proofs.
