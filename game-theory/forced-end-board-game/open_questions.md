Open Questions and Edge Cases:

1. Is a draw truly impossible for all values of m ≥ 3?

2. Can a player force an early loss before the board
   becomes nearly full?

3. Does the forced-end behavior always occur,
   or are there exceptional board sizes?

4. Can the existence of a forced-end position
   be proven formally for all m × m boards?

5. How does the behavior change if the winning row length
   is not exactly m − 1?

6. Would restricting symbol choice (fixed symbols per player)
   change the outcome?
